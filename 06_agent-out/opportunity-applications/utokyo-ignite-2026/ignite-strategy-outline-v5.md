I'm not silently picking a side: the 職務経歴書 outline below is built to supply concrete, person-sized operational evidence alongside the strategic material Adler wants to lead with, so both halves of the posting's ask get answered.

**Tension B — one lifework project vs. five concurrent commitments.**
Candidate profile #3 describes a half-commit candidate built around *one* ongoing "lifework" project. Adler's own point 4 names five current/upcoming roles he wants IGNITE to synergize with. Naming all five in a single ~1000-character field risks reading as already over-committed. My Q7 outline caps this at two, but which two is Adler's call.

Profile #3 specifies a mechanism: stabilizing half one's annual income through the center. Nothing in Adler's own words addresses income or time split — his framing is about reciprocal learning and synergy, not financial mechanics. This is a gap, not a contradiction; I route it to Q12 as a live negotiation item rather than assert it as settled in Q7.

**Tension D — a 3+ year commitment expectation vs. a time-limited entry route.**
Profile #4 wants someone in their late 20s/early 30s prepared to build a team and stay 3+ years. No briefing states Adler's willingness or ability to commit to that horizon. The only entry-route fact on record is that he applied for a Japan working-holiday visa — inherently time-limited — and the job posting separately notes no information exists on visa/tax treatment of 業務委託 (contractor) status for a foreign national based partly outside Japan. **Tension E — academia/research framing vs. "not a research position."**
Adler's second grouping is built around Minerva, Thiel, JASA and IACR board membership. The posting states plainly this role is classified as research/education support staff, explicitly not research. I'm not suppressing the academia material — the posting's own duties include research into the program's own design effectiveness, and its skill checklist recognizes conference presentations and research on innovation ecosystems as legitimate categories. The redirection below is to present JASA/IACR as organizational service, in Adler's own words, not as a scholarly-seniority claim.

**Named and set aside, not a tension:** Adler's age-stamped achievements (MOEA training at 16, Fubon award at 17, etc.) are a factual description of who else was in those specific programs — not a claim about other IGNITE applicants. Nothing in the governing rules forbids them; the "no competitive differentiation" instruction is about not ranking against other candidates for this job, which this isn't. I've kept the age-stamped facts as the evidentiary spine for "did this work early and continuously" and dropped only the evaluative aside ("while peers were 30+"), as a tone edit — appropriate for a form that describes itself as non-comparative ("any of the following or a combination is welcome"), not as a resolution of a source conflict.

(No briefing states when the filmmaking work began — don't add a specific age.)
