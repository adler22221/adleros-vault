### What to carry across

- Concrete scene or fact first, concept second. He never opens with an abstraction.
- Long clause-chained sentences alternating with short flat ones. Not uniform staccato.
- Explicit logical connectives carrying one thought into the next: しかし / このため / なぜならば / その代わりに / ですから.
- Direct statements of intent in the first person: 「〜たいと考えています」「〜と思います」. He says what he wants plainly.
- Reason-giving: 「〜からです」.
- Katakana technical terms sit naturally inside argument (フィードバック、エコシステム、ワークショップ).
- No decorative adjectives, no rhetorical flourish, no self-congratulation.

### What NOT to carry across

His unedited Japanese contains non-native constructions (「〜とは同じ」「当てはめないので」「通じで」). **Do not reproduce these.** Write correct Japanese in his rhythm. The goal is his voice, not his typos.

Avoid spoken-address endings in a written form: 「〜させてください」「〜たいです」 read as speech. Prefer 「〜たいと考えています」.
