## §1 Host Institution Overview
The employer is the 越境イノベーション研究センター (Cross-Border Innovation Research
Center), an endowed center to be established October 2026 within the University of Tokyo
Graduate School of Engineering, in collaboration with the Graduate School of Humanities and
Sociology (文学部/人文社会系研究科). It is funded by Sony Group's endowment
"ソニーグループ人材育成基金" (Sony Group Human Development Fund). The center operationally
grows out of, and manages, the IGNITE program (formally: 東大発イノベーター着火プログラム,
publicly branded "Ignite Your Ambition" / IGNT), which itself is run day-to-day through a
一般社団法人 (general incorporated association) called "Ignite Your Ambition," established
2021-06-07, with its registered office at UTokyo's Faculty of Engineering Building 2.
IGNITE's own site describes itself: "IGNITEは、テクノロジー×ビジネス×アートのスタートアップ
手法を未来構想と社会実装を通じて身に付ける、東大発のイノベーター着火プログラム。" (IGNITE is
a UTokyo-originated innovator-ignition program where participants acquire technology ×
business × art startup methodology through future-visioning and social implementation.)
[Source: ignite-your-ambition.com homepage]

IGNITE was originally launched in 2019 as a Sony-UTokyo social-collaboration course; per the
program's own framing, after "七年間の試行錯誤" (seven years of trial and error) it is being
substantially scaled up starting in 2026 — which is the direct context for this large,
10-person staff hiring round. [Source: WebSearch synthesis of ignite-your-ambition.com and
related coverage, cross-referenced across two independent queries]

## §2 Research Agenda & Active Themes
This is an entrepreneurship-education program, not a conventional academic research center
in the humanities/social-science sense — its "research" component (per call.md) is oriented
toward program-design effectiveness research (研究on the program itself), not a disciplinary
research agenda. From the fetched content, the program's active thematic priorities are:

  business, and art — explicit three-way partnership between UTokyo, Tokyo University of the

- Individual-level "ignition" of personal drive/passion — the program's central metaphor is
  "火" (fire): finding, naming, and fanning each participant's own motivating "fire."
- A stated new institutional turn (2026 relaunch) toward embedding this as a durable
  UTokyo research center rather than a single Sony-sponsored course, with an explicit stated
  interest in cultivating PhD-track talent toward eventual UTokyo faculty positions (per
  call.md, verified from the JREC-IN posting itself, not independently found on the IGNITE
