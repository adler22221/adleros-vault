# Strategy & Outline v8 — UTokyo IGNITE

This supersedes [[ignite-strategy-outline-v7]]'s Q6 section only. Q7 and Sections 4–9 are
unchanged from v7 — see that document.

**What was wrong with v7's Q6, stated plainly:** its claim sentence made the field's turning
point a recognition that Joann's current research field, 当事者研究, mirrors what Adler was
doing as a teenager filming his own school. That recognition is about a parallel between his
past and her field — it says nothing about IGNITE. But きっかけ asks specifically what triggered
interest in *this posting*. A vivid scene that doesn't answer the question it's attached to isn't
a stronger version of the field, it's a non-sequitur dressed as one. This happened because the
"open on a concrete scene, not a stated thesis" instruction got followed correctly as craft, but
nobody checked afterward that the scene's content still did the field's actual job. That check is
applied here.

The fix keeps what v7 got right — a real scene instead of a flat statement, the visa/health fact
resolved rather than evasively flagged — and restores the one piece of correct logic that was
present all the way back in the very first draft of this field, before any of this session's
rebuilding: the actual recognition that belongs here is Adler reading the posting's own
description of who it wants, and seeing himself in it. 当事者研究 still appears — it's a true,
relevant fact about who Joann is now — but as texture establishing her, not as the field's
central claim.

## Section 2: Q6 — 本募集に興味を持ったきっかけ (past-facing, ≤1000 JP characters)

**One-sentence claim:** The trigger is direct: a former mentee, now working in Japan's research
world, saw this specific posting and thought of him — and when he read it himself, what actually
stopped him was recognizing himself in the posting's own description of the person it's looking
for.

**Ordered beats:**
1. Open on the concrete moment of receiving her message, not a flat statement that a referral
   happened. **Needs Adler's real memory, not an invented one:** what her message actually said,
   how she framed it, where he was when he read it.
2. Establish who she is, briefly, as texture rather than as the field's engine: a mentee he
   worked with as a teenager, now doing 当事者研究 — research led by the people an issue actually
   happens to — in Japan. This is where that fact belongs; it explains why her judgment carries
   real weight (she's seen his mentoring firsthand and now works inside the kind of institution
   this posting is at), not why he was drawn to IGNITE itself.
3. *(The actual recognition beat — this is what carries the field's weight, and it needs to be
   about the posting, not about Joann.)* State plainly what stopped him when he read the posting
   himself: its own description of the person it wants — someone who was an early member of a
   venture or initiative, still runs it, doesn't want to give it up, and genuinely wants to
   mentor the next generation on a half-commit basis. That's a close description of his actual
   situation, in the posting's own words, and recognizing that is the real trigger.
4. The visa/health fact, resolved rather than flagged, carried forward from v7: state plainly
   what the working-holiday visa permits for paid hours across the trial, then one clause of
   consequence — what the forced pause actually changed in what he now treats as urgent. No
   diagnosis. **Needs Adler's real answer, not a guess:** the actual visa terms, and the specific
   thing the pause changed.
5. Close by connecting the standing intention — deepening his Japan grounding, the Dojo as an
   already-moving cross-cultural practice — as continuation of the recognition in beat 3, not as
   a fresh, disconnected list of plans.
