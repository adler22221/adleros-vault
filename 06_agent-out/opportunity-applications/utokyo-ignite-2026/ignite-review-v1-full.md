# Review v1 (full): 東京大学 越境イノベーション研究センター — 教育スタッフ・メンター
Reviewer: Agent 04 (selection-committee simulator) · Reviewed: draft-application-v1.md
Date: 2026-08-02 · Deadline: 2026-08-09

Scoring is adapted to the document type per the orchestrator's instruction: this is a Japanese
job application (履歴書 + 職務経歴書 uploads + a Google Form questionnaire), screened on
documents and then a 30–60 minute interview, with 10 trial hires and 3–5 conversions in
March 2027. Rubric weights are unchanged; three dimensions are re-specified —
thematic alignment = fit against the five 求める人物像 bullets; activity specificity =
concreteness and verifiability of *claimed* experience; logistics clarity = honesty and
adequacy of the Q4/Q12 disclosures.

Per instruction, the ten 要確認 placeholders are treated as correctly-handled gaps and are not
marked down as such. One of them (要確認①) is assessed as handled *wrongly* — see §3 H1.

## §1 Persona and Lens

**The reviewer is the Sony-side chief producer, not the academic.** I have chosen the producer
persona deliberately. The evidence is in the instrument itself: the 求める人物像 bullets are
written in operator/HR register (アクセラレーター, BizDev, ロジ手配, 成績評価, ハーフコミット,
年収の半分), the screen is an 18-item checkbox grid that is obviously a work-allocation
instrument across ten contractors, and the program's public CTA
(「AIには決して描けない、あなたの偏愛やモヤモヤ、情熱をぶつけてください」) is a producer's
copy, not a professor's. 中尾's 失敗学 lineage sets the center's intellectual weather, but the
person filling ten October slots through a 一般社団法人 is 杉上-type: Sony People Solutions,
seven years of 試行錯誤 behind him, a center that does not legally exist yet, and a program
launching in eight weeks.

He prioritises **who can be in the room in October** over who is intellectually impressive. He
would be immediately enthusiastic about a founder who is not asking to be rescued by the job —
bullet 3 exists because he has been burned by people who wanted a UTokyo affiliation more than
they wanted the work. He is tired of 就活 polish, of 「情熱があります」 with nothing attached,
and of applicants who signal they are above the 告知 and the 参加者管理. He reads the second
document (職務経歴書) before the essays, and he reads it 減点法 — looking for what will fail in
month two. A secondary academic lens applies only to bullet 5 and the §8 block.

## §2 Strongest Elements

1. **「読んでいて手が止まったのは、求める人物像の三つ目です。…自分のことが書かれている、と
   思いました。」** (Q6) — this is the correct opening move and almost nobody in the pool will
   make it. It proves he read the actual form, it names the bullet by ordinal rather than
   quoting it back decoratively, and it converts a generic 志望 into a specific recognition.
   Preserve verbatim.
2. **「撮影の大半は、同じ年頃の人たちが自分の問いを見つけたり、手放したりする場面でした。」**
   (Q6) — the single best sentence in the application, and the only one in it that could not
   have been written by anyone else. It is the program's 火 without ever using the word 火, and
   「手放したり」 is the fire going out — precisely the 偏愛/モヤモヤ the CTA asks for. It is
   also correctly left un-moralised: no 「その経験から私は…」 tacked on. Preserve verbatim, and
   protect it from any editor's instinct to explain it.
3. **「どちらも畳みません。」** (Q7) — four characters of actual spine, in native operator
   register, answering the ¥500,000 question before it is asked (shared core #1). The whole
   half-commit paragraph is the strongest strategic passage in the document.
4. **The numbers block** (Q7 ¶2) — 1,000名以上/8か国, 200名規模, 1,000万台湾ドル申請書から報告
   まで, 4,000名超のうち300件以上. Four named programs, four numbers, no adjectives. This is
   what 「持ち込めるのは」 should look like.
5. **「制度の枠組みが現場の運営をどこで縛るのかを両側から見てきました。」** (Q7) — bullet 2's
   高い視座 half delivered in one clause without a single buzzword, correctly placed *after*
   the 泥臭さ material per bullet 2's own ordering. Short and second, exactly as specified.
6. **Q12 in full** — three items, no more, the 在留資格 risk disclosed without a promise
   attached, and item 3 (what happens when a participant's project stalls) is a practitioner's
   question that gathers Q001 intelligence without betting on either register. 「着任時期を軽く
   見積もりたくないので、先にうかがう次第です」 is correct Japanese register and correct
   posture. This field is finished; do not touch it.
7. **要確認⑩ (the lapsed JASA board term) was caught and the tense corrected.** Credit where
   due — an expired 理事 term silently left in the present tense would have been the kind of
   small factual failure that a 減点法 reader treats as diagnostic.

## §5 Line-Level Flags

- **「泥臭い仕事は、私が誰かに任せてきた仕事ではありません。」** → asserts a disposition
  before earning it, and is the closest thing in the draft to the cliché fit-judgment names.
  Also 仕事…仕事 in one sentence. → Open the paragraph with the fact (道道教育, dates, cadence,
  完走), and let the disposition be inferred.
- **「告知も参加者管理も成績評価も、同じようにやります。」** → a promise standing in for

  本気の目標の一つに置いています。」** → 「も含めて」＋「の一つ」＋教員(≠教授) = wanting credit
  for a claim while avoiding it. → One unhedged sentence naming the 教授 horizon directly, per

  つもりです。」** → introduces 定年 and the idea of leaving into an application about starting;
  answers an objection nobody raised. Also 腰掛け／腰を据える repeat 腰 within ~200字. →
  Delete. The 3年以上 statement two paragraphs earlier already carries the durability claim.
- **「確かめた上で、続けたいと思っています。」** → 〜たいと思っています as the final verb of the

- **「業務は原則オンラインで対応し、必要に応じて東京への渡航が可能です。」** → the least

## §6 Voice Check

Against profile/writing-samples.md, the picture is mixed in an interesting way, and the draft
is mostly on the right side of it.

Adler's English samples (IRE cover letter, portfolio About) run long, clause-stacked,
abstraction-first, and fond of coinages — "education curation hub," "serial and synergistic
intrapreneurship," "systems connector and prototyping hub." That register, translated
literally, would have produced exactly the translationese this application needed to avoid.
**It largely did not happen.** The Japanese here is shorter-breathed than his English, and the
best passages are ones his English samples contain no equivalent of: 「どちらも畳みません。」
「七年かかりました。」「手が止まった」「回した」「腰掛け」. 回した and 畳む are operator
vocabulary a translated-from-English draft does not reach for. On the whole this reads as a
fluent N1 writer with real exposure to Japanese professional and academic settings — which is
the correct impression and a true one.

Residual translationese, all flagged in §5: 「日本国内の都道府県ではありません」 (obvious
