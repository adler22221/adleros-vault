# IGNITE — University of Tokyo Cross-Border Innovation Research Center
## Education Staff & Mentor (東大発イノベーター着火プログラム IGNITE)
Source: https://jrecin.jst.go.jp/seek/SeekJorDetail?id=D126061862
JREC-IN posting ID: D126061862
Verified via two live WebFetch passes on the JREC-IN listing, 2026-07-26.

## Institution / Program
University of Tokyo (東京大学), Faculty of Engineering, Cross-Border Innovation Research
Center (越境イノベーション研究センター) — the "IGNITE" (東大発イノベーター着火プログラム)
entrepreneur-education program.

## Position
Education Staff and Mentor for IGNITE. JREC-IN's own classification for this listing:
"研究・教育補助者相当" — research/education *support* staff equivalent (explicitly not
classified as a research position).

## Duties (as stated)
Workshops, event coordination, participant management, logistics, grade evaluation, and
research on program design effectiveness. The posting frames the ideal candidate as able to
both execute tactical tasks and strategize program improvements. Also named: optional
accompaniment on Yamanashi fieldwork trips and student chaperoning ("山梨へのフィールドワーク
の同行、学生の引率") — not previously captured; aligns with Adler's regional-revitalization
track record (NDC Qian-Tsao model, Soochow student group to Tokyo).

## Mindset section (【マインドセット】, staff-specific — re-verified 2026-08-06)
Not previously captured from the posting. Three points, stated for this hire specifically:
- **守破離**: absorb ~7 years of the center's accumulated practice ("守") before attempting to
  innovate on it — an explicit sequencing expectation, not a one-time culture-fit line.
- **超長期の視点**: no fixed dissolution date for the center; hires are expected to help build
  succession systems so the center keeps evolving after they personally leave.
- **Team structure**: joining an existing ~6-person mentor team (stated as mid-40s), while also
  building a new 4–5-person team alongside the other trial hires.
This leans collaborative/succession-oriented rather than lone-founder-hero — bears directly on
open-questions.md Q001 (culture-fit concern), which should be marked substantially resolved in
this direction, not left as an unresolved risk.

## Employment type
業務委託 (outsourced contractor status — not direct employee status).

## Compensation and structure
- 3-month trial: October 1 – December 31, 2026. Approximately 50 hours total workload.
  ¥500,000 (pre-tax) for the trial period. Verbatim: "3か月で約50時間の業務量で50万円税別の
  業務委託・外注"
- Continuation: only 3–5 of the 10 trial hires will be offered continuation — a half-time
  role at ¥3,000,000–5,000,000/year, starting April 2027. Verbatim: "3〜5人程度を本採用予定"
  / "300〜500万円のハーフコミット". Not guaranteed.
- Continuation is explicitly framed as a 5+ year horizon, not just the first post-trial year:
  "5年以上にわたり共に歩む" (re-verified 2026-08-06). Post-trial half-commit on-site cadence is
  stated as 週2〜3日程度 — distinct from and higher than the trial-period 週1,2日 figure below;
  do not conflate the two when drafting long-term-commitment language.
- Standing cadence during the trial: weekly ~30-min online check-in ("週に1回30分程度の
  オンライン定例"), otherwise self-paced.

## Number of positions
10 for the trial phase (募集人員: "10名").

## Eligibility / qualifications
- Bachelor's degree required; master's or doctorate NOT required. Verbatim: "学士があれば、
  修士や博士は不要"
- Business experience in entrepreneurship, corporate ventures, or social enterprises welcomed
  ("起業・社内起業・社会起業などの事業経験"), and mentorship experience with startups/
  entrepreneurs preferred.
- PhD holders interested in eventually becoming University of Tokyo faculty are explicitly
  and particularly welcomed — framed as a talent pipeline.
- No explicit statement on foreign-national or language eligibility in the posting itself.

Document screening, then a 30–60 minute interview (in-person or online):
"書類選考後、対面またはオンラインで30～1時間の面談"

## Timeline
- Application deadline: 2026-08-09
- Interview/decision: mid-September 2026 (results by ~mid-September)
- Start date: 2026-10-01 ("2026年10月01日"), though early applicants may be able to start
  earlier by mutual agreement: "早めに応募いただいた方から面談し、場合によっては着任時期の
  前倒しも検討"

## What this is NOT
Not a research position, not a tenure-track or tenured academic appointment, not a
guaranteed path to anything beyond the 3-month trial. The University of Tokyo brand and the
PhD-holder pipeline language are real, but the actual role scope is program-support and
mentorship-facilitation work for an entrepreneurship-education program.
