# Strategy & Outline v7 — UTokyo IGNITE

This supersedes [[ignite-strategy-outline-v6]]'s Q6 and Q7 sections; Sections 4–9 (職務経歴書,
Q10–Q13, open items) carry forward from v6 unchanged, since this round's scrutiny was scoped to
Q6/Q7 only. What changed: Adler read v6 and judged it too shallow — a checklist of true, relevant
facts that could describe several different strong candidates, not something singular to him.
That was tested properly rather than argued about: three independent critics (a jaded Sony-side
HR screener, a UTokyo faculty reviewer checking for actual thinking versus just biography, and a
startup-world peer checking for what would actually be remembered) scrutinized v6's Q6/Q7 outline
against the harshest reasonable bar. All three converged on the same verdict — it doesn't stand
out — and converged on the same two specific failures: the visa/health beat in Q6 raised a real
operational question (can he legally work paid hours during the trial?) without answering it,
which reads as evasive rather than tactfully private; and Q7's use of 守破離 was flagged by every
critic as the single most predictable line available — quoting the posting's own term back at it
is exactly the kind of AI-shaped move the posting's own call explicitly says it doesn't want.

Three sharper rebuilds were then generated from different angles (leading with a concrete scene,
leading with a real unresolved tension, leading with texture over abstraction) and judged against
each other and against v6. The judge picked a clear direction — the scene-first version, with one
piece merged in from another version and one cut the judge made unprompted — rather than hedging
across all three. That's what's below.

## Section 2: Q6 — 本募集に興味を持ったきっかけ (past-facing, ≤1000 JP characters)

**One-sentence claim:** The loop doesn't close because Joann refers him to the posting — it
closes in the instant he recognizes that what she now calls 当事者研究 (research led by the
people an issue actually happens to) is the same thing he was doing at fourteen, filming his own
school before either of them had a word for it.

**Ordered beats:**
1. *(Carries most of the field's weight — open inside the moment of recognition, not the moment
   of notification.)* He learns her term for what she now does — 当事者研究 — and the beat renders
   the instant that term lands as a description of his own unnamed teenage practice, not as a
   fact about her career. **This needs a real detail from Adler, not an invented one:** what he
   was actually doing when it landed, and what specifically in her phrasing snagged him. Without
   that, this beat collapses back into exactly the abstraction it's meant to replace.
2. One subordinate clause, not a headline: it's this same person, now doing that work formally
   inside Japan's academic world, who told him about this posting.
3. The visa/health fact, resolved rather than flagged. State plainly what the working-holiday
   visa actually permits for paid hours across the three-month trial, then one clause of
   consequence — what the forced pause actually changed in what he now treats as urgent. No
   diagnosis, ever. **Two things this needs from Adler directly, not a plausible guess:** the
   actual visa terms as they apply to this specific paid arrangement, and the specific thing the
   pause actually changed for him.
4. Close by returning to beat 1's recognition rather than opening a new list — the standing
   intention (the Dojo, a cross-cultural 当事者-centered practice already in motion) framed as
   continuation of that instant, not a fresh inventory of plans.

## Section 3: Q7 — 志望動機・意気込み (future-facing, ≤1000 JP characters)

**One-sentence claim:** His resolve is to bring the actual friction of his own history — fifteen
years arguing that institutional gatekeeping trims people down to fit its own criteria, now
asking to work inside one of the most selective institutions there is — into IGNITE's daily
work, under one stated rule for when to hold back and when to push.

**Ordered beats:**
1. Operational commitment, anchored to one concrete artifact instead of a category: the NT$10M
   National Development Council grant ZA Share won, and the 30,000–60,000 participants the
   platform runs annually — facilitation, participant management, and logistics evidenced by
   this, not merely claimed in the abstract.
2. One named case, not a statistic, replacing the earlier "sixteen documented mentee outcomes"
   line: the crew member from *If There is a Reason to Study* who left school and became an
   award-winning YouTuber on rural sustainability without a university degree — the moment that
   person chose the non-institutional path. **This needs Adler's actual memory, not an assumed
   one:** what his own role in that specific transition actually was, concretely, not just that
   he was the mentor of record.
3. The real friction, named directly rather than resolved away by a borrowed phrase: fifteen
   years spent building an argument (Allocation Dependence) against institutional gatekeeping,
   now applying to work inside one of the apparatuses that argument is about — stated plainly, no
   softening clause. Paired with his own condition for resolving it, in place of the earlier
   守破離 pledge: he doesn't propose changing a format until he's run it exactly as built through
   one full cycle and can say specifically why it's built that way. **Explicit cut, made by the
   judge reviewing this version:** do not add anything about a long-term aim at a faculty
   position inside this same institution here — for a fixed-term, ten-slot trial role, stating
   that aim converts the application into "using this as a stepping stone" and hands a reviewer a
   motive question nothing else in the field defends. The friction is complete without it.
4. Reciprocity, kept to one line, no elaboration: IGNITE as a place to contribute to and learn
   from.
5. Cross-pollination: TIAL as the named anchor, the Dojo (the cross-cultural Taiwan/Hong Kong/
   Japan/Korea practice from Q6) named as an existing and emerging connection. ZA Share and
   KASpaces are not repeated here — they're already carried by beat 1.
