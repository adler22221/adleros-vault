5. **博士号取得意欲 stated accurately, once, in Q7 only** — 論文博士 (doctorate by published

## Strategy 1: 「自分の火を持ったまま、次の世代に火をつける」 — The Half-Commit Practitioner

*Axes: Framing = Biographical narrative | Entry point = His own founding/lifework experience
| Positioning = Peer practitioner who already has his own thing | Tone = Curious practitioner
| Anchor bullet = 3 (with 2 and 4 fully carried in the shared core)*

**Core Claim**: Adler is the person bullet 3 describes — someone who was an initial member of
what he built, is still building it, and wants exactly the half-commitment the center is
offering, because educating the next generation of innovators is not a detour from his
lifework but the same work at a different scale.

**Entry Point**: Awakening Cooperative Lab (founded 2012, still running, 3,000+ stakeholders
across Taiwan/Hong Kong/China) and the East Asia Dojo (2025–, currently being launched, with
members in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Japan, Korea and Vietnam) — a founded-and-still-running lifework
plus a live 立ち上げ中 project, which is the exact two-state condition bullet 3 names.

**The Bridge**: The center is not looking for someone whose career this job completes. It is
looking for someone whose career this job *fits alongside* — that is what 「ハーフコミットで
両方に挑戦したい方（年収の半分を本センターで安定させたい方）」 means, and it is unusual enough
that most applicants will have to argue for it. Adler does not. He also arrives with the thing
that makes half-commitment safe for the employer rather than risky: a decade of running
programs to a schedule with other people's money and other people's students. The 火 metaphor
is answered honestly here — he is not claiming to ignite people, he is claiming to have kept
his own fire lit for seventeen years while helping others find theirs, with 16 individually
mentored cases and documented outcomes.

**What it does to the application**:
- **Q6 (きっかけ)**: origin-led. Where he saw the posting, and why it landed on prepared
  ground — the 2026 Japan decision window he was already navigating, and the recognition of
  his own situation in bullet 3.
- **Q7 (志望動機・意気込み)**: leads with the half-commit statement (shared core #1), then
  what he brings, then 泥臭さ, then the 3-year and doctorate sentences.
- **Q10**: full breadth per §Q10 below.
- **職務経歴書**: opens with the founder/operator identity, not with credentials.

**Risks**: The weakest strategy for cluster-2 comparison — a reviewer benchmarking against
startup mentors sees social-sector founding and may under-rate it, since Adler has no
accelerator or VC-side experience (Q10 items B and Q). Bullet 3 also carries a subtle trap:
"I want half my income stabilised" is what the form asked for, but written badly it reads as
"I want a subsidy for my real work." The sentence must land as *both*, not as *instead*.

**Strength**: This is the only strategy where Adler is describing rather than arguing, which
is exactly what the program's own CTA (「AIには決して描けない、あなたの偏愛やモヤモヤ」) asks
for and what a generic, AI-polished application cannot fake. Appeals most to the
practitioner/producer-type reviewer — the Sony-side program people who built this center
around the same instinct.

**Pitch Draft**: "I founded and still run an education lab I started at nineteen, and I'm
launching a cross-East-Asia research community now. I'm not looking to stop — I'm looking for
exactly the half-commitment you describe: half my income stable here, my own work alongside,
and my hands on your students' projects."

## §Q6 and §Q7: the two 1000文字 fields

These are the real persuasive surface and the pre-form analysis did not know they existed.
They are **not** the same question, and the single most common failure mode will be writing
one essay and splitting it in half. Agent 03 must not draft these until Adler picks a strategy
and resolves the bullet-5 decision (fit-judgment.md, final section).

### Q6 本募集に興味を持ったきっかけ (1000文字以内, required)

**What it is**: the *trigger*. Backward-looking, factual, narrative. The literal question is
"what made you interested in this recruitment," and the literal answer includes *how he came
across it* — which most applicants will skip and which is the honest beginning of the story.

**What it should do**:
- Name the concrete trigger: seeing the posting (JREC-IN / the program's own 2026-07-01 news
  post), and what about it stopped him.
- Explain why it landed on prepared ground rather than as a cold discovery: the Japan
  throughline (Tekisen 2017–19, N1 2019, Japanese academic associations, the 2025 Tokyo
  work), and the live 2026 decision window about basing part of his life in Japan.
- Carry exactly one controlled personal beat — the honest origin of his own 火. He started
  making a documentary about his own education system at fourteen and finished it at
  twenty-one; that is the least generic sentence available to him and it is the one that
  answers 「AIには決して描けない、あなたの偏愛やモヤモヤ」 without saying the word 情熱.
- End by handing off, not by concluding. Q6 should leave the motivation *unspent*.

**What it must not do**: rehash the CV (the uploads do that); assert knowledge of the
program's internals he does not have; open with 「貴センターの理念に共感し」; use 越境 or 着火
decoratively; make the case for hiring him (that is Q7).

**Register**: plain, specific, chronological. Closer to 700–850文字 than to a padded 1000.

### Q7 志望動機・意気込み (1000文字以内, required)

**What it is**: *motivation and resolve*. Forward-looking. What he wants from this, what he
will give, on what terms, and for how long. 意気込み is a commitment word — it expects a
statement of what he is prepared to do, not a description of how he feels.

**What it should do**, in roughly this order (strategy determines what leads; the shared core
determines what must appear):
1. The half-commit statement — bullet 3, in his own words, as an honest description of his
   situation rather than a quotation of the form (shared core #1).
2. What he brings, mapped tightly enough to the Q10 boxes that the checklist reads as
   substantiated rather than optimistic — three or four named programs with numbers, not a
   list.
3. The 泥臭さ sentence, with a recent specific attached (shared core #2), and the altitude
   claim immediately after it, short (shared core #3).
4. The horizon: 3+ years (bullet 4), and the 博士号取得意欲 sentence — 論文博士 route, intent
   only (shared core #5). **The professorship sentence is a separate, optional insert** that
   Adler switches on only if he resolves the bullet-5 question affirmatively; Agent 03 must
   write the doctorate sentence so it stands alone without it.
5. One closing line of actual resolve. Not a summary.

**What it must not do**: repeat Q6's story; explain or apologise for the absence of a PhD;
name a theoretical framework; promise the October 1 start as logistically settled; name Nakao,
Nagato or Sugiue.

**The clean separation rule**: Q6 is past, trigger, facts. Q7 is future, offer, terms. If a
sentence could sit in either, it belongs in Q7 — Q6 can afford to be thinner, Q7 cannot.

**Register**: same voice as Q6 but denser and more committal. Use most of the 1000文字.
